# Legal & Acceptable Use Policy

## Scope and Intended Use

Arrmes is a local natural-language routing and policy-verification framework for self-hosted API gateways. It does not host, index, distribute, or provide access to any media content.

## Open-Content Fixtures

All documentation examples, test fixtures, and synthetic sandbox entities (`100`- through `400`-series cohorts) use synthetic identifiers or Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY) open movies (*Sintel*, *Tears of Steel*, *Big Buck Bunny*, *Cosmos Laundromat*) and CC0 recordings (*Open Goldberg Variations*).
